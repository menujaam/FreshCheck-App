FreshCheck Prototype

FreshCheck

A clickable prototype of the app design. It runs on sample data and does not use a camera or a trained model yet.

  1. Log in. Press Log in with the fields empty to see the failed login.
  2. Scan: pick a food, open the camera and capture any photo. On a phone this opens the camera. Each capture gives the next result: fresh, then use soon, then spoiled.
  3. Inventory: search, open an item, or tap the "use sooner" banner to go to recipes.
  4. Recipes: choose ingredients (spoiled food is locked out), swipe the ideas, open one and mark it as used.

The pH cut-offs and the remaining days are placeholders. The real values will come from my food experiment. The result does not come from the photo yet, because the colour-to-pH model is not connected. Freshness labels are indicative, not safety guarantees.
